# DarGlobal (Dar Global PLC) — source: https://darglobal.co.uk (scraped 2026-11-19)

## Overview
DarGlobal is a London-listed (London Stock Exchange, listed 2023) luxury real estate developer, investor and asset manager. Tagline: "Global capital. Enduring places." Markets: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Spain, UK (plus Greece, Maldives, US presence). Over three decades (31-32 years) of luxury development experience. Headline figures (Q3 2026): $23B gross development value (GDV), 7 international markets in the homepage stat (FAQ/investor pages cite 14 cities in 9 countries and 9 global sales offices), 12+ global brand partners. CEO and founder: Ziad El Chaar. Sales offices: New York, London, Marbella, Athens (coming soon), Dubai, Jeddah, Riyadh, Muscat, Doha.

## Asset management and tokenization
Dar Global Capital Partners (DGCP) is the DFSA-regulated investment management subsidiary headquartered in DIFC, Dubai; deploys capital in equity, debt and hybrid instruments across GCC, European and US markets. Tokenization: blockchain-based fractional ownership of premium properties, with liquidity via regulated platforms.

## Why invest and residency
Saudi Arabia: invest SAR 4M (~$1.07M) for residency; 30-year corporate tax exemption; SAR pegged to USD.
Oman: real estate investment can give lifetime residency; 0% personal income tax.
Qatar: no personal income or property tax; permanent residency at QAR 3.65M ($1M); investor visa from QAR 728,000 ($200,000).
UAE: 10-year Golden Visa from AED 2M (~$545,000) including off-plan; 0% income tax and property tax.

## Buying process
Choose property, review plan with advisors, reserve securely, sign digitally or via offices, complete payment and registration. Typical documents: passport copy, contact info, reservation form, payment details. Flexible instalment plans; international buyers welcome with full ownership rights; many branded residences are sold furnished.
