# Wasalt (وصلت) — source: https://wasalt.sa (scraped 2026-11-19)

## Overview
Wasalt is a Saudi-based real estate platform, described as Saudi Arabia's first AI-backed real estate platform, for verified properties and trusted brokers. Users can buy, rent, sell, invest, or join auctions. Licensed by the Real Estate General Authority (REGA), license 1200016591; licensed by the Ministry of Tourism, license 73106008 (category: accommodation and unit reservation); CR number 7017195509. Apps: Wasalt and Wasalt Business.

## Services
Buy and Rent listings: apartments, villas, land across Saudi Arabia.
Projects: verified real estate projects for investment.
Daily Rentals: owners list short-term rentals with weekly earnings, free coverage and low commission.
Barter (new).
Get Matched / Matchmaker: share your requirements and get connected with relevant property experts and brokers.
Auctions: Infath (enforcement) auctions and private auctions covering real estate, vehicles and movable assets.
International luxury properties: exclusive listings from global developers.
Post a property for sale or rent in a few steps.

## Coverage
Cities: Riyadh, Jeddah, Makkah, Madinah, Dammam, Al Khobar, Khamis Mushayt. Popular Riyadh districts: Al Rimal, Al Olaya, Al Yasmeen, Al Khaleej, Al Nakheel. Searches include villas and apartments for sale or rent, and land for sale.
