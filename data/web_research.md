# External web research on Dar Global and Wasalt (retrieved 2026-11-19)

## Dar Global PLC listing on the London Stock Exchange (February 2023)
Source URL: https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1623969
Source type: external web (Argaam)
Dar Al Arkan announced the listing of its international development subsidiary Dar Global on the London Stock Exchange on 23 February 2023.
Dar Global was established in 2017; the parent Dar Al Arkan Real Estate Development Co. is listed on Tadawul (Saudi Arabia).
Liberum Capital Limited acted as capital markets adviser and Norton Rose Fulbright as legal adviser.
At listing Dar Global had 11 premium projects in six countries, including downtown Dubai, Muscat, Costa del Sol and Central London.
Three projects (Urban Oasis, Da Vinci Tower in Dubai, Sidra in Bosnia) were due for completion in Q4 2023.
The LSE ticker is not stated in this source.

## Dar Global PLC listing structure and valuation
Source URL: https://www.agbi.com/articles/dar-al-arkan-subsidiary-completes-600m-london-listing/
Source type: external web (AGBI, as summarised via search; also Arab News and Dar Global press)
Dar Global began trading on the London Stock Exchange main market on 28 February 2023, described as one of the first Saudi homegrown brands listed on the LSE main market.
Reported valuation at listing was SAR 2.25 billion (about US$600 million).
It was a direct listing combined with a private placement before admission that raised about US$72 million gross; the parent could sell shares without Dar Global issuing new stock as in a traditional IPO.
Shares were admitted to the Standard Segment of the FCA Official List and the LSE Main Market.
Source summaries did not state the ticker; the investegate and lse.co.uk RNS pages list Dar Global announcements under the code DAR.

## Dar Global PLC ownership and relationship with Dar Al Arkan

## Dar Global FY2023 results (year ended 31 December 2023)
Source URL: https://www.lse.co.uk/rns/DAR/full-year-results-for-the-year-ended-31-dec-2023-76udhwwmyget5g1.html
Source type: external web (London Stock Exchange RNS via lse.co.uk; cross-checked with Zawya and Gulf News)
Dar Global reported FY2023 revenue of US$360.6 million, up 351% from US$80.0 million in 2022.
Profit before tax was about US$81.2 million versus a loss of US$5.2 million in 2022 (another report gives US$81.3 million).
Gross profit was US$146.4 million, a 41% margin.
Contracted sales were 1,498 units worth about US$1,025 million (about 46% of launched GDV of about US$2.2 billion).
Portfolio GDV was US$5.9 billion across 12 active projects; the company targeted at least US$700 million cumulative revenue across FY2024 and FY2025.
A release headline cited a 23% return on equity for 2023.

## Saudi foreign property ownership law (market context)
Source URL: https://www.jll.com/en-sa/insights/saudi-arabia-foreign-ownership-legislation-goes-live
Source type: external web (JLL, with King & Spalding and GT Law summaries)
Saudi Arabia's new Real Estate Ownership by Non-Saudis law was published on 25 July 2025 and took effect after 180 days, around 21 to 22 January 2026 depending on source.
It replaces the 2000 law, covers non-Saudi individuals and entities, links ownership to designated zones and generally prohibits ownership in Makkah and Madinah.
On 23 June 2026 the Council of Ministers approved implementing regulations and designated areas (GT Law).
Dar Global's CEO cited a strategic foothold in Saudi Arabia ahead of its opening to foreign investors (AJ Bell, 11 March 2026).

## Wasalt Infath electronic auction licence
Source URL: https://sharikatmubasher.com/news/article/21375099
Source type: external web (Sharikat Mubasher)
