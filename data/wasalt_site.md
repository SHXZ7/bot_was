## Wasalt FAQ - Auctions and Matchmaker
Source URL: https://wasalt.sa/en
What is Wasalt Auctions? It is a feature where real estate assets are sold through timed auctions; bidders compete to determine the final sale price. Auctions include assets from private sellers and those in collaboration with Infath (Entrustment and Liquidation Center).
What is Matchmaker and how does it work? Wasalt Matchmaker helps you find potential leads based on your live listings. It matches leads based on the property type, purpose, district and category of your live property. Once your listing is live, Matchmaker shows leads that match those preferences so you can connect with the right leads.

## Wasalt blog - How to buy a house in Saudi Arabia (2026)
Source URL: https://blog.wasalt.sa/en/how-to-buy-a-house-in-saudi-arabia/
The Wasalt blog states homeownership in Saudi Arabia rose from 47% in 2016 to 66.24% in 2025, and Vision 2030 targets 70% by 2030.
First-home buyers can get government-backed financing through the Real Estate Development Fund (REDF) via the Sakani programme, with a maximum LTV of 90% for first-home buyers.
First-time buyers purchasing homes worth SAR 1 million or less may qualify for a Real Estate Transaction Tax (RETT) exemption; the article says Sakani had served more than 1.4 million beneficiary families as of early 2026.
The article says the debt burden ratio cannot exceed 45%, and advises getting pre-approved before choosing a property. Foreigners can buy in designated zones under the 2026 law; ownership in Makkah and Madinah remains restricted.

## Wasalt blog - foreigners buying property in Saudi Arabia 2026
Source URL: https://blog.wasalt.sa/en/foreigners-buying-property-in-saudi-arabia-2026/
The Wasalt blog says that starting January 2026 Saudi Arabia broadened foreigners' access to property ownership under a new law, subject to REGA conditions.
Foreign investment of at least SAR 4,000,000 (about USD 1,070,000) can lead to long-term residency via the Golden Visa; the property must be residential, completed, free from mortgages or liens, and valued by a TAQEEM-registered valuer.
The new law allows freehold and usufruct rights; ownership zones are designated in cities like Riyadh and Jeddah, with stricter rules in Makkah and Madinah. Foreign residents may own one residential property outside designated zones for personal use; foreign companies may acquire real estate for business operations or employee housing.
Tips: Iqama should have at least six months validity. The article also mentions a 5% Real Estate Transaction Tax (RETT).
